## 1. TOTAL ADDRESSABLE MARKET (TAM)

### 1.1 Market Definition
The total global market of retail investors who could potentially benefit from AI-powered SEC filing summaries.

### 1.2 Top-Down TAM Calculation

#### Base Population Data
- **US Retail Investors (2024):** 40M+ individual investors (Broadridge study)
- **US Stock Market Participation:** 58% of Americans invest in stocks
- **US Adult Population:** ~260M adults
- **Total US Investors:** ~150M Americans with stock market exposure

**However, we need active retail investors who make individual stock decisions:**
- Active retail investors managing individual stocks: 40M (Broadridge)
- Average holdings per investor: 8 individual stocks (doubled from 4 in 2018)
- Investors who research fundamentals: 60% (40% follow herd behavior per research)
- **Core US TAM:** 40M × 60% = **24M investors**

#### Global Expansion Markets
**International investors who invest in US stocks:**
- UK retail investors with US stock access: 15M+ (AJ Bell, ii, Barclays data)
  - Estimated 20% invest in US stocks: 3M
- Canadian retail investors: 10M+
  - Estimated 40% invest in US stocks: 4M
- Australian retail investors: 8M+
  - Estimated 30% invest in US stocks: 2.4M
- European investors (Germany, France, Netherlands): 20M+
  - Estimated 15% invest in US stocks: 3M

**Global TAM (International):** 12.4M investors

#### Filtering for Fundamental Analysis Users
Not all investors read SEC filings or care about fundamental analysis:
- Percentage who conduct fundamental analysis: 60% (per research data)
- Percentage who would value time-saving tools: 80% of fundamental investors

**Adjusted Global TAM:**
- US: 24M × 60% × 80% = 11.52M
- International: 12.4M × 60% × 80% = 5.95M
- **Total TAM:** 17.47M investors

### 1.3 Bottom-Up TAM Calculation

#### SEC Filing Universe
- **SEC Registered Companies (2024):** 7,902 reporting issuers
- **US-domiciled exchange-listed:** 3,929 companies
- **Annual 10-K filings:** ~7,900
- **Quarterly 10-Q filings:** ~23,700 (3 per year per company)
- **8-K current reports:** ~40,000+ annually (event-driven)
- **Form 4 insider trading:** ~80,000+ annually

**Total meaningful filings annually:** ~150,000 filings

#### Investor Coverage Demand
- Average investor tracks: 8 companies (per Broadridge data)
- Annual filings per company: ~19 (1 × 10-K, 3 × 10-Q, ~15 × 8-K/Form 4)
- Total filing notifications per investor annually: 152 filings

#### Market Size by Pricing
**Conservative pricing ($15/month, $180/year):**
- 17.47M investors × $180 = $3.14B TAM

**Mid-range pricing ($20/month, $240/year):**
- 17.47M investors × $240 = $4.19B TAM

**Premium pricing ($30/month, $360/year):**
- 17.47M investors × $360 = $6.29B TAM

### 1.4 Value-Based TAM Calculation

#### Time Savings Value Proposition
- Average time to read a 10-K: 2-3 hours
- Average time to read a 10-Q: 1-2 hours
- Average time to read 8-K: 30-60 minutes

**Annual time investment for 8 companies:**
- 8 × 10-K × 2.5 hours = 20 hours
- 24 × 10-Q × 1.5 hours = 36 hours
- 120 × 8-K × 0.75 hours = 90 hours
- **Total:** 146 hours per year

**Value calculation:**
- Median investor income: $54,000/year (per research)
- Effective hourly value: $25-$50/hour
- Annual time saved value: 146 hours × $35/hour = $5,110
- Willingness to pay for 10% of time value: $511/year

**Reality check:** Newsletter pricing must be 5-10% of value created
- Reasonable annual price: $240-$360 (80-95% discount to full value)

**Value-based TAM:** 17.47M investors × $300 average = **$5.24B**

### 1.5 Final TAM Consensus

**Conservative TAM Estimate:**
- Investors: 17.47M
- Revenue: $3.14B - $4.19B annually

**Optimistic TAM Estimate:**
- Investors: 40M (including all US retail investors)
- Revenue: $9.6B - $14.4B annually

**Recommended TAM for Planning:**
- **Investors: 23.2M** (midpoint between conservative global and full US market)
- **Revenue: $4.18B - $6.96B** (at $180-$300/year pricing)
- **3-Year CAGR:** 8-12% (aligned with retail investor growth trends)

## 2. SERVICEABLE ADDRESSABLE MARKET (SAM)

### 2.1 Market Filters Applied

The SAM represents the portion of TAM that tldrSEC can realistically serve given product constraints, go-to-market strategy, and competitive positioning.

### 2.2 Geographic Filter
**Initial Market Focus:** US, UK, Canada, Australia (English-speaking, strong US stock access)

- US investors: 11.52M (from TAM)
- UK investors: 1.44M (from TAM)
- Canada investors: 1.92M (from TAM)
- Australia investors: 1.15M (from TAM)

**Geographic SAM:** 16.03M investors (69% of TAM)

### 2.3 Behavioral & Demographic Filters

#### Filter 1: Active Individual Stock Holders
- Must own 5+ individual stocks (not just index funds)
- Percentage of investors: 60% of retail investors
- **Filtered to:** 9.62M investors

#### Filter 2: Willingness to Pay for Financial Tools
- Must subscribe to at least one paid financial service/newsletter
- Industry data: 25-30% of investors pay for tools
- **Filtered to:** 2.89M investors

#### Filter 3: Email-Native Investors
- Must regularly use email for investment research (vs. only mobile apps)
- Percentage: 90% (high email adoption in finance)
- **Filtered to:** 2.60M investors

#### Filter 4: Age & Digital Literacy
- Age range 25-65 (peak earning/investing years)
- Comfortable with AI-powered tools
- Percentage: 85% of current investors
- **Filtered to:** 2.21M investors

#### Filter 5: Minimum Investment Account Size
- Minimum $10,000 in individual stocks (justifies $180-$360/year spend)
- Percentage of investors: 70% (based on income data)
- **Filtered to:** 1.55M investors

### 2.4 Competitive Landscape Adjustment

**Existing Alternatives:**
- Bloomberg Terminal: $24,000/year (institutional, not retail)
- Seeking Alpha Premium: $240/year (20M visitors, estimated 100K-500K paid)
- Motley Fool Stock Advisor: $199/year (500K-1M subscribers)
- AlphaSense: $1,200+/year (institutional focus)
- Traditional analyst reports: $50-200/report

**Market Gap:** No dedicated SEC filing summarization tool for retail investors at accessible price points.

**Competitive SAM adjustment:** 90% available (10% locked in multi-year contracts with competitors)
- **Filtered to:** 1.40M investors

### 2.5 Product-Market Fit Filter

**Ideal Customer Profile (ICP):**
- Investors who read 1+ SEC filings per quarter currently
- Value time savings over deep financial expertise
- Trust AI-powered tools for initial analysis
- Prefer email delivery over app-based platforms

**ICP percentage of filtered market:** 50% (aggressive filtering)
- **Final SAM:** 700K investors

**However, market education and freemium model can expand this:**
- Many investors don't read filings because they're too time-consuming
- AI summarization creates new behavior (latent demand)
- **Adjusted SAM with latent demand:** 1.40M × 1.2 = **1.68M investors**

### 2.6 Final SAM Consensus

**Conservative SAM (Existing Behavior):**
- Investors: 700,000
- Annual Revenue: $126M - $252M (at $180-$360/year)

**Moderate SAM (Some Latent Demand):**
- Investors: 1,400,000
- Annual Revenue: $252M - $504M (at $180-$360/year)

**Optimistic SAM (Full Latent Demand + Market Expansion):**
- Investors: 2,320,000
- Annual Revenue: $418M - $835M (at $180-$360/year)

**Recommended SAM for Planning:**
- **Investors: 2.32M** (10% of TAM - realistic for focused GTM)
- **Revenue: $418M - $696M** (at $180-$300/year average pricing)

## 4. MARKET VALIDATION & STRATEGIC INSIGHTS

### 4.1 Is This Market Venture-Backable?

**YES - Strong venture case based on:**

1. **Market Size:** $4.2B TAM, $418M SAM is sufficient for $100M+ ARR potential
2. **Growth Rate:** 8-12% CAGR in retail investor population (secular tailwinds)
3. **Unit Economics:** LTV:CAC ratio of 7-13:1 is exceptional
4. **Competition:** Fragmented market with no dominant player in SEC filing summarization
5. **Defensibility:** AI model improvement, data network effects, brand moat
6. **Path to $100M ARR:** Achievable with 5% SAM capture at $250 ARPU

### 4.2 Market Growth Drivers

**Positive Tailwinds:**
1. **Demographic Shift:** Gen Z and Millennials entering peak earning years
   - Average investor age: 33 years (down from 45+ in 2015)
   - 77% of Gen Z started investing before age 25

2. **Democratization of Investing:**
   - Zero-commission trading (Robinhood effect)
   - Fractional shares enabling smaller investments
   - Social media investment communities (Reddit, Discord)

3. **AI Adoption Acceleration:**
   - Retail investors increasingly comfortable with AI tools
   - ChatGPT moment has normalized AI-powered analysis

4. **Information Overload:**
   - SEC filings becoming longer and more complex
   - Average 10-K now 50,000+ words (vs. 30,000 in 2000s)
   - Time scarcity drives demand for summarization

5. **Regulatory Transparency:**
   - SEC increasing disclosure requirements
   - More frequent 8-K filings for material events
   - Climate, cyber, and DEI disclosures adding complexity

**Headwinds to Monitor:**
1. **Market Volatility:** Bear markets reduce retail investor activity
2. **AI Commoditization:** Risk of OpenAI/Google offering free summarization
3. **Regulatory Changes:** Potential changes to SEC filing requirements
4. **Competition:** Established players (Seeking Alpha) adding AI features

**Current Competitive Landscape:**

**Market Gap Identified:**

- AI-native approach is differentiated from legacy content companies

**Competitive Moat Strategy:**
1. **Data Network Effects:** More users → better training data → better summaries
2. **Brand Trust:** Become synonymous with "SEC filing summaries"
3. **Speed:** First-to-email within minutes of filing (time-sensitive moat)
4. **Personalization:** ML-powered relevance ranking for each user's portfolio

### 6.4 Key Risks & Mitigation

**Risk 1: AI Commoditization**
- **Threat:** ChatGPT or Google offers free SEC filing summarization
- **Mitigation:**
  - Speed moat (real-time summaries within 15 minutes of filing)
  - Personalization (portfolio-specific insights, not generic summaries)
  - Email delivery (lower friction than copy/paste into ChatGPT)
  - Brand trust (users trust specialized tool over general LLM)

**Risk 2: Low Conversion Rates**
- **Threat:** Freemium conversion stays at 2% instead of 4-8%
- **Mitigation:**
  - Optimize free tier (1 ticker cap creates urgency to upgrade)
  - Email drip campaigns showing value (social proof, time saved)
  - Limited-time promotions (50% off first year for early adopters)
