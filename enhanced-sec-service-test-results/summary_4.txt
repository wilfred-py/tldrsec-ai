Based on my review of Tesla's 10-K filing, here is a summary of the key points most relevant to investors:

Key Financial Results & Metrics:
- Tesla achieved record vehicle deliveries of 1.31 million in 2022, up 40% year-over-year. 
- Total revenue grew 51% to $81.5 billion, with automotive revenue increasing 52% to $71.5 billion.
- Operating income more than doubled to $12.6 billion, with operating margin improving to 16.8%.
- Earnings per share increased to $3.64 from $2.26 in 2021.

Significant Business Developments:  
- Began limited production and deliveries of the Tesla Semi, its first electric truck.
- Announced plans for Cybertruck and new Roadster electric vehicles.
- Continued global expansion of manufacturing with new Gigafactories in Austin, Texas and Berlin, Germany.
- Advanced full self-driving technology and artificial intelligence capabilities.
- Expanded insurance product to 12 states with plans for further growth.
- Opened Supercharger network to non-Tesla vehicles in some locations to accelerate sustainable energy transition.

Risk Factors & Challenges:
- Increasing competition in the electric vehicle market from established automakers and new entrants. 
- Evolving regulations and incentives related to electric vehicles, self-driving, and clean energy.
- Ongoing supply chain challenges for components and raw materials.
- Macroeconomic uncertainty from COVID-19 impacts, inflation, interest rates etc.

Outlook & Guidance:
- Tesla believes increasing production capacity and localizing production will further improve affordability. 
- Continues to target 50% average annual growth in vehicle deliveries over a multi-year horizon.
- Remains focused on reducing manufacturing costs and ramping up higher-margin software offerings.
- Optimistic the Inflation Reduction Act will accelerate electric vehicle and clean energy adoption.

Notable Changes:
- Automotive gross margin increased to 27.4% from 25.6% as average selling price and production efficiency improved.
- R&D expense increased 36% to $3.5 billion as Tesla invests in product roadmap and key technologies. 
- Tesla ended 2022 with over $22 billion in cash and cash equivalents.

In summary, Tesla delivered very strong financial results as it continues to rapidly scale production and deliveries. While it faces increasing competition and some macro uncertainty, the long-term outlook remains positive as Tesla expands its product portfolio, improves affordability, and benefits from policies accelerating the shift to sustainable energy.
