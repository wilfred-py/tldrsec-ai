## Recommendations

1. **OpenRouter Provisioning Key**: Request a provisioning key from OpenRouter to enable programmatic access to usage history for automated cost reconciliation.

2. **Cost Propagation Fix**: The `costUSD` field in JobQueue shows `0` while Summary records show actual costs. Update the summarize handler to propagate costs to JobQueue.

3. **Dec 15 Email Remediation**: Consider a one-time remediation to send the 17 Dec 15 summaries that were generated but not emailed.

4. **Automated Reconciliation**: Implement daily reconciliation between OpenRouter activity and database records to catch discrepancies early.
